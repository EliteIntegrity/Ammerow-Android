# AMMEROW™: Lands Beyond for Android

**A free, single-player roguelike where the descent is only half the journey.
Built for touch.**

The lands beyond have been cut off. The village endures, the roads are dangerous,
and beneath it all the Meridian leads towards the Upwelling. Pack for an
expedition, choose your risks, and try to bring something back.

This is the Android edition of Ammerow, the same game as the Windows release:
**Angband's turn-based combat and permanent death**, with its own peoples,
creatures and relics, an overworld to explore, and connected caves seen from the
side. You play it in landscape with a d-pad, buttons that follow the moment and
gestures made for phones and tablets. Physical keyboards and game controllers
work too.

## Plan the journey. Survive the return.

- **Make each turn count.** Classic grid-based combat, spells, equipment and
  dangerous encounters reward preparation and knowing when to retreat.
- **Descend into the Meridian.** Explore a hundred-level main descent through
  procedurally generated dungeons, gathering knowledge and strange relics along
  the way.
- **Take another route underground.** Explore connected side-view caves. Climb
  rock, hammer pitons, deploy ropes, peer into shafts and manage stamina and air.
  Doors, traps, chests, creatures and tunnelling make the journey more than a climb.
- **Fish for more than supper.** Watch fish approach your hook, adjust your line,
  and bring the catch home to eat, sell or donate to the village larder.
- **Choose your adventurer.** Different peoples and classes, magical practices,
  equipment and procedurally assembled artefacts give each run its own character.
- **Keep the decisions, ease the repetition.** Explore runs until something needs
  you: a monster, new loot, an injury. A tap anywhere stops it. Fighting stays
  entirely in your hands.

## Made for touch

- **The obvious action, one tap away.** The large button follows the moment:
  Explore, Get, Shop, Down, Open, Fish, or Fire again at the same target. Rest,
  Look and your ranged attack sit beside it.
- **A d-pad that stays under your thumb.** Eight directions; keep your thumb
  down to keep walking, and the centre waits a turn. Tap a square to walk there,
  hold one for its menu, and pinch to zoom.
- **Your own quick bar.** Pin a potion, a scroll, a spell or any command to a
  slot. Item slots show how many you have left.
- **Items answer with buttons.** Highlight an item and its uses appear: Quaff,
  Read, Wield, Throw, Inspect and Pin.
- **Put it down at any moment.** The run saves whenever you leave the app;
  Continue picks it up.
- **Phones and tablets.** Any landscape screen shape, an interface size to suit
  you, and a field guide written for touch.

## The dungeon is not the only way down

The caves have their own geography: ledges, deep shafts, rock formations and
underground pools. Your route back matters as much as your route in. In a cave
the buttons become Jump, Grip, Piton, Rope and Peek, with Open or Disarm beside
a door, chest or trap.

Beside water, the large button reads **Fish**. The d-pad moves the hook Out, In,
Deeper and Shallower; when a fish bites, the large button becomes **Strike!**

[![The fishing view over the village pond: BITE - STRIKE NOW, a line in the water and the Strike! button.](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ammerow-Android/releases/download/v0.1.3-android.1/ammerow-android-06-fishing.png)](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ammerow-Android/releases/download/v0.1.3-android.1/ammerow-android-06-fishing.png)

## Read the world your way

Choose **ASCII**, **Hybrid** or **Full Tile** at any time in Settings,
Appearance. ASCII offers Square and Classic cells; Hybrid draws creatures and
items as sprites over glyph terrain; Full Tile adds textured terrain in
**32×32 or 64×64**, with a **Keep floors ASCII** option. Pinch the map to zoom;
menus and messages keep their own size.

## Look before you commit

Tap **Look**, then **Next** and **Prev** to step through what you can see.
Creature cards combine character-art portraits with descriptions and what your
adventurer has learned. **Target** marks a creature for your next shot, and
**Recall** shows all you know of it.

## Start playing

1. On your device, open the
   [latest release](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ammerow-Android/releases/latest)
   and download `Ammerow-<version>.apk`, not GitHub's automatic **Source code**
   archives. Requires Android 8.0 or later on a 64-bit ARM or x86_64 device.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager
   to install unknown apps, then install.
3. Choose **New Run**. **Help** opens the field guide, **More** lists every
   command, and **Menu** holds the settings.

To update, install the new APK over the old one: your runs and settings are
kept. **Don't uninstall first**, as that deletes your saves. Saves written by a
newer version may not open in an older one. Each release lists SHA-256
checksums in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Ammerow is in active Early Access development.

## Build, contribute and explore the source

This repository holds the complete source of the Android edition: the game in
`game/`, which is the desktop edition's published source with the changes listed
in [ANDROID-CHANGES.md](ANDROID-CHANGES.md), and the Android app in `app/`. A
build from it plays with plain glyphs and synthesised sound effects; official
APKs add the separately licensed artwork and audio. Screenshots are release
attachments; the asset pack is not stored in this repository.

See [DEVELOPING.md](DEVELOPING.md) for building, installing, the tests and how
the app fits together, and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before proposing
changes.

Every official APK carries its exact corresponding source as
`assets/source/Ammerow-Android-source.zip`, and each release attaches the same
archive with the sources of the third-party libraries built into the app. The
repository front page can be updated independently of those frozen release
snapshots.

**AI disclosure:** Ammerow and its Android edition were developed with AI
assistance under human direction and review, including code, writing and visual
assets. Illustrations and sprites derive from image-generated source artwork;
sound effects use code-driven procedural synthesis, and music was composed
through AI-assisted code and virtual instruments. No AI service or live AI
generation runs while you play. The asset details are in
[game/lib/art/NOTICE.md](game/lib/art/NOTICE.md),
[game/lib/tiles/NOTICE.md](game/lib/tiles/NOTICE.md) and
[game/lib/sounds/ammerow/NOTICE.md](game/lib/sounds/ammerow/NOTICE.md).
