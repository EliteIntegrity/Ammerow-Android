The Known World and Travel
==========================

Ammerow's launch basin is a deliberately compact three-by-three surface.  The
village, roads, woods, lake and cave approaches are real local maps joined at
their edges.  Green arrows mark a physical route out of the current region.
Stepping onto a travel arrow crosses to its connected region and teaches
that direction of the route, whichever way you approached it.

The known-world screen
----------------------

On the surface, open it with Travel, beside the large button while
nothing is in view, or under More (away from stairs; on an up staircase,
or underground, Travel climbs instead).  It is also under More,
Commands, Action, Known world / travel.  The d-pad moves its cursor
one map cell.  '@' marks your current surface region, 'v' marks the
surface above your underground location, '*' is a discovered region, and
'?' remains unknown.

OK travels to a selected discovered region only after its route has been
learned in the required direction.  Fast travel is refused while threatened,
restrained, affected by an unsafe temporary condition, or already being moved
by another effect.  It advances world time, hunger and light fuel.  It does not
simulate or animate every step of the journey.

Entrances and the Meridian
--------------------------

Standing on a down entrance, the large button reads Down (or Enter, for a
cave mouth or hole).  The Meridian is the hundred-layer descent that anchors
the first release.  Satellite caves can hold different rewards or connect to
a side-view cave through a marked hole.  Ordinary trap doors never count as a
side-view entrance.

Cavewood Cavern is one top-down area.  Its exits lead back to Cavewood or
down the shaft into the Deepwell's side-view caves; it has no ordinary
down staircases to lower dungeon floors.  Look names these entrances for
their destinations.  Cavewood Cavern's mossy green rock and earthy floor
contrast with the Meridian's cool slate walls.  Green Y trees on the
surface can be walked through, but their foliage blocks sight and
missiles; ordinary grey rock is still solid.

Only sites physically discovered by this character are named on the map.  The
small launch basin is a complete expedition boundary, not the claimed full
extent of Ammerow; later regions can attach through the same route system.
