Fishing
=======

Stand beside eligible water: the large button reads Fish.  Press it to
start.  Fishing opens across the playfield while leaving the surrounding
world visible.  Opening and leaving are free; every rod movement, line
movement, wait, strike and reel is a normal turn.  Creatures and hazards
therefore keep acting while you fish.  You need a rod; the village Fishing
Shop sells them.

Waiting for a bite
------------------

The d-pad is labelled while you wait:

  Out          extend the rod one column
  In           retract the rod one column
  Deeper       lower the hook one depth row
  Shallower    raise the hook one depth row

The large button is Wait (hold position for one turn), and Leave stops
fishing.

Fish move before a stationary hook can receive a bite.  Moving the hook cannot
also cause a bite on that turn.  A longer rod reaches farther and deeper water,
where more valuable fish may occur.  After a long quiet spell, the nearest fish
which can live at the hook's depth begins moving visibly toward it.  It must
still cross the water and occupy the hook's exact cell before it can bite, so
casting near fish matters.

Bite and winding
----------------

When BITE appears the large button becomes Strike!.  Press it, or Wait for
one turn; a missed moment lets the fish escape.  After a successful strike
the large button becomes Reel: press it once per turn, or keep your finger
on it to keep reeling.  The fish may pull line back before it is landed.

A catch becomes an ordinary carried item which can be eaten or sold.  To feed
the village, carry fish into the General Store and use Donate under More,
then choose the catch and quantity.  Rain Minnows, Mudbellies, and Glassfins
contribute 1, 3, and 6 portions respectively.  Securing the first 20-portion
emergency reserve makes Expedition Rations available from the General Store.
The Fishing Shop keeper knows where a full-depth Yew Longline was lost.  Check
the visible map and message history before casting: the water is not a pause
screen.

Rain Minnows and Mudbellies give 1 XP when caught and another 1 XP when
donated; Glassfins give 2 XP for each.  Donation XP counts fish, not portions:
giving a stack at once pays the same as giving its fish one by one.  These
small rewards continue after the larder unlock.  Selling fish gives gold,
not XP.

Rare catches
------------

Fernscale lives only in forest pools, at line depths 7-11.  Bring an Ash
Water Rod or better.  Its green fin and copper stripe are distinctive: a
catch can be eaten, sold, or donated for 3 portions.  Catching and donating
each give 1 XP.

Cistern Bream lives only in ruined cisterns, at line depths 10-16.  An Ash
Water Rod reaches the shallow end; a Yew Longline reaches the whole band.
Expect a stronger pull.  Eat it, sell it, or donate it for 6 portions; 2 XP
for each catch and each donation.

Blind Deepfish is rare in the deeper side-view cave pools: at least three
section descents below the root cavern.  It lives at line depths 13-16,
requiring a Yew Longline.  Lowering the line in a shallow cave section will
not find one.  It sells well, provides a generous meal, or contributes 10
portions; catching and donating each give 3 XP.  It does not replace
Glassfin in Sound the Underflow.

All three remain optional.  Your existing catches and village quest still
work as before.  Fishing and selling can fund supplies, but take turns,
pack space and a journey back to a willing shop.  Inspect a catch (Inv,
then tap it) for its habitat and uses.

One-time village requests
-------------------------

The larder cook has three optional requests.  Donate one Fernscale for 40
gold, one Cistern Bream for 120, or one Blind Deepfish for 240.  All are
available from the start: no need to accept them or finish the emergency
reserve first.  In the General Store press Donate, then choose a fish and
quantity.  Sell sells instead; selling or eating a fish does not fulfil a
request.

The donation receipt announces the gold as well as the usual portions and
XP.  Each request pays once per character, even if you donate a stack.
Keep surplus fish for food, sale, or further donations at the normal
portions and XP rate.  These are small thank-you gifts, not repeatable
bounties or required progression.  Char, then Quests, shows the habitats,
rewards and which requests are complete; the d-pad's up and down choose a
request.

Older characters can fulfil these requests too.  Earlier catches and
donations are not counted retrospectively; only a new donation fulfils a
request.

Cave pools
----------

In a side-view cave, stand securely on an air tile directly beside the pool;
when the position is valid the status line says so and the large button
reads Fish (Jump moves beside it).  You cannot cast while falling,
climbing, hanging, or swimming.  Fishing actions use the cave's normal action cadence, so nearby
cave creatures continue to act; a catch which does not fit in the pack
falls onto the cave tile at your feet.
