Interface, Settings and Saving
==============================

Saving
------

The game saves your run whenever you leave the app: switching away, the
Home button, or the screen turning off.  If Android closes the app while
it is away, the run is waiting under Continue when you return.  To save
and stop, use Menu, then Save and Return Home.

Settings
--------

Menu, then Settings.  The pages are:

  Appearance              theme, map font, ASCII treatment, and the map
                          presentation: ASCII, or Hybrid, which draws
                          monsters and some items as portrait sprites
                          over ordinary ASCII terrain, at 32 x 32 or
                          64 x 64 detail (a glyph stands in wherever
                          there is no sprite).  Detail does not change
                          the map's scale; cards and shopkeepers stay
                          coloured ASCII.
  Interface               the menu and message font, interface density,
                          the statistics and message overlays, and where
                          recent messages appear (top right unless you
                          choose Top or Bottom)
  Sound                   the master volume and each kind of sound
  Display / Performance   map zoom, animated combat, and whether the
                          weather is shown (it chooses itself; its
                          sound is under Sound)

Tap a setting to choose it, and use the d-pad's left and right to change
it.  Back returns to the list of pages.

Character creation
------------------

On the origin, class and attribute-method choices, Options opens the
birth options: tap an option, or press Toggle, to switch it.  They are
fixed once the run starts, and options marked experimental stay so.

Attribute allocation shows each attribute's base value, the origin and
class modifiers, the final value and the points spent, with the focus,
carrying weight, blows and gold that result.  The d-pad's left and right change
the highlighted attribute; Reset starts again.  With the roller, Reroll
rolls a new candidate and Previous brings back the last one; colours and
+/- marks compare the final values.  Attributes take effect at
thresholds, so not every point changes everything.

Zoom
----

Pinch the map with two fingers, or use Zoom in and Zoom out under More.
Map zoom is separate from the size of menus and messages, so a dense menu
does not make the map tiny.

Look
----

Look (on the right-hand buttons, or under More) shows what is on a
square; move it with the d-pad and press Back to close the card.  A
target you chose may keep its marker after an attack, but its card
closes when you finish aiming; Look shows it again.  Holding
a finger on a square opens a menu that includes Look At.  In a cave, Peek
is a different safety action, described in 'spelunking.txt'.

Shops
-----

Tap an item to highlight it: Examine shows it at once, and Buy (or a tap
on the highlighted item) opens its actions.  Sell offers your own items.
In the General Store, Donate gives a fishing catch to the village larder.
When nothing you carry is wanted, the reason stays above the stock until
your next action.

Poison
------

While you are poisoned the statistics panel says so beside your hit
points, as well as in messages and the status line.  It clears when the
poison ends.

Learning and using magic
------------------------

Formula, prayer, practice and ritual are the different realms' names for
book magic.  Learning one does NOT make it a passive ability or set it
working.

  Learn magic   (under More, or on a book in your pack) learn magic from
                a book, when your class has a study slot free
  Use magic     (on the right-hand buttons, under More, or on a book)
                choose its book, then the learned magic
  Browse        (under More, or on a book) read a book without using it

Carry the book so it is there when you need it.  Outside caves a book
on the floor at your feet also serves; in a cave it must be in your pack.  The list shows the level needed, the Focus each attempt
costs, the chance of failure and whether you have learned it.  Details
hides or shows the descriptions.  You can highlight anything in the list
to read about it, but only eligible magic can be used or learned: "Not
learned" means you must leave the list and Learn magic first; being the
right level is not enough.  An attempt takes time and Focus even when it
fails.  Without enough Focus you can risk overexertion, which may make
you faint or harm your Constitution.  Backing out of the list is free.

Remove Hunger is a one-off top-up to roughly half fed when you are below
that.  It does nothing if you are already at least half fed, and does
not slow digestion afterwards: use it again when you are hungry.  A
protection such as Resist Poison likewise lasts only for its duration
once used.  By contrast an item's Slow Digestion works by itself while
worn; learning magic has nothing to do with it.

Abilities (under More) describes your origin and class traits and when
they apply.  It is not where learned book magic is used.

Battery and performance
-----------------------

The game draws only what changes and sleeps while it waits for you.
Hiding the weather under Display / Performance helps on older devices;
it changes nothing in play.  Some uncommon screens still use the
original text layout; they work the same way, with the d-pad, OK and
Back.
