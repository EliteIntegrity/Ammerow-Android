Touch Controls
==============

The buttons are in three places.  Their positions never change; only the
labels on the right-hand buttons follow what you are doing.  The rule for
those: they are what you can do with whatever is in focus, best first -
the square you stand on, the item, spell or square you have highlighted,
or the question you are asked.

The side column
---------------

At the screen's edge (on the camera's side, if the screen has one):

Menu    the in-game menu: settings, help, save and return home
Inv     your pack
More    every command, in groups; tap one, or Done to close; hold one
        to put it on the quick bar.  Commands at the end opens the
        game's own menu of every command.
Help    this field guide
Map     the whole level
Log     earlier messages (or tap the recent messages at the top right);
        Find searches them
Char    your character dossier; Next page (or the d-pad's left and right)
        turns its pages, and Rename renames you

These work from inside other screens too: the screen closes first.
Pressed again while its own screen is open, Inv, Help, Map, Log and Char
close it.  On Home and the screens reached from it, and while creating a
character, only Menu and Help are shown.

The d-pad
---------

Slide or press in any of eight directions to walk one step.  Keep your
thumb down to keep walking.  The centre, marked Wait, waits a turn; keep
your thumb on it to keep waiting, until something comes into view.  In a
cave, waiting on stable ground is how stamina comes back.  In menus and
lists the d-pad moves the highlight, and left and right change tabs.
It is drawn faintly so the map and text show through it, and a touch
just outside its ring still counts as the d-pad.

The right-hand buttons
----------------------

On the map the large button does the obvious thing where you stand:

  Wait      wait a turn, when nothing else applies
  Get       pick up what is here
  Down, Up  take the stairs
  Enter     go down into a cave mouth or hole
  Shop      go into the shop whose door you are on
  Fish      start fishing beside water
  Open      open the door next to you

Around it are Rest (as long as needed), your ranged attack and Look (or
Wait, when the large button is busy with something else).  On the
surface, Travel (the known world) takes the ranged attack's place until
something comes into view.  Explore, which walks to the nearest
unexplored spot, is under More, with Up, Down and Travel.

When a monster comes into view, the large button becomes your ranged
attack: Fire (with a launcher and ammunition for it), otherwise Use magic
(once you have learned some), otherwise Throw (a throwing weapon or a
flask of oil).  Each asks which ammunition, magic or missile, then where:
Nearest, At target (once you have one), Choose, the d-pad, or a tap on
the monster.  Without a ranged
attack the large button is Wait: let the monster come to you.  Look and
what your square offers sit beside it; Rest goes, since resting stops for
a monster in sight.  To fight hand to hand, walk into the monster.

Once you have fired, used magic or thrown, the large button does it
again - Fire again, Use again or Throw again, with what it uses written
under it: the same ammunition, magic or missile at the same target, in
one tap.  When that target has gone it asks where (Nearest is the large
button then).  Fire, Use magic or Throw beside it starts afresh, to
choose other ammunition or another target.  Fire nearest (under More, and can go on
the quick bar) fires the first ammunition in your quiver at the closest
monster without asking.

Just after the game changes the large button, it ignores taps for a
moment, so a tap meant for the old action cannot trigger the new one.

Your pack and equipment
-----------------------

Inv opens your pack; the d-pad's left and right show your equipment,
quiver and the floor.  Tap an item to highlight it, and the buttons
become what you can do with it: the large one is its use (Quaff, Read,
Eat, Aim, Wield or Wear, Take off, Fire, Pick up...), next to it are Back,
Inspect and one more, and the rest (Drop, Throw, Inscribe, Ignore...) are
in a row beside the d-pad.  A dimmed one cannot be done just now, such as
aiming an empty wand.  Tap the highlighted item again for the full list
in the game's own menu.  After Inspect you come back to the list.

The quick bar
-------------

The row of slots along the bottom of the map (in a cave, just above its
status lines) holds what this character keeps reaching for.  To fill a
slot:

  an item   highlight it in your pack, equipment or quiver and press Pin:
            its main use (Quaff, Read, Eat, Aim, Wield, Take off, Fire,
            Throw...) goes on the bar
  magic     highlight it in a book's list when using or browsing it and
            press Pin
  anything  under More, hold a command

Tap a slot to use it; the game asks only what it must, such as a
direction.  An item slot shows how many you have, and greys out when you
have none.  Hold a slot to empty it.  The small button at the bar's left
shows more rows.  Each character keeps their own slots.

Looking and targeting
---------------------

Look shows what is around you.  Move its cursor with the d-pad, Next and
Prev, or tap a square, and the buttons follow what it is on:

  a monster  Target (to fire or cast at it later with At target), Recall
             (what you know of it), Next
  an item    Walk to, Next, Prev
  a place    Walk to, Next, Prev

When choosing a target to aim at, the large button is Target.  The card
describing the monster, item or place shows only while you look or
choose; afterwards your target keeps its highlight on the map.

Magic
-----

Use magic or Learn magic shows the book's list: tap an entry, or
highlight it and press Use (or Learn).  Details hides or shows what each
does.  Learning magic does not set it working: use it from its book each
time you need it (see 'interface.txt').

Touching the map
----------------

Tap      walk to that square by the shortest known path; a tap next to
         you steps or attacks there.  Squares you have not seen yet are
         ignored, so a finger that misses a button does not send you
         wandering into the unknown (the d-pad or Explore go there)
Hold     open the game's menu for that square; on yourself it lists
         every action (use, cast, rest, stairs, pick up and more)
Pinch    zoom the map in and out

Back
----

Android's Back button is the game's Escape: it cancels a prompt, closes a
screen, or opens the in-game menu.  With the More drawer open it closes
the drawer.

Prompts
-------

The buttons change for each kind of question:

  -more-               Next (or Skip)
  a card               (the opening story, a quest briefing, a
                       donation's receipt) OK, Back or a tap goes on
  menus and lists      tap a row, or OK, Back, PgUp, PgDn
  which item?          tap it (or move to it with the d-pad and press OK)
  yes or no            Yes, No
  how many             the digit buttons, then OK; All takes everything
  a direction or aim   the d-pad fires that way; Nearest aims at the
                       closest enemy; Choose picks a target; At target
                       uses the one you already chose; or tap a monster
  a name               OK, Random, or Type... for the on-screen keyboard

The on-screen keyboard appears only when you ask for it (Type..., or
Keyboard under More) and closes when the prompt ends.

Shops
-----

Tap an item to highlight it; Buy and Examine act on it.  Tap the
highlighted item again for the game's own menu for it (Examine, Buy,
Buy one).  Sell offers your own items: tap the one to sell.  Purchases
and sales ask Yes or No.  At home the buttons are Take, Stash and
Examine.  In the General Store, Donate gives your fishing catch to the
village larder; a card then shows what it added, the XP it earned and
the village's progress.

Starting a run
--------------

New Run, then choose each part of your character, or press Random for a
random choice and Auto to finish the whole character at random.  Leave
the name blank and press OK (or press Random) for a random name.  Options
(beside the d-pad) shows the birth options.  Attributes are bought with
the d-pad's left and right (Reset starts again), or rolled: Reroll for a
new roll, Previous to bring back the last.

After a character dies, New Run can offer a new character based on the
previous one: Use as is, Redo, Change name, or Options.

Load Run lists your saved runs: OK loads the highlighted one, and Delete
removes it after asking Yes or No.  A character who has died is not
listed there.

Home also offers Lore (the world, its peoples, and Classes & Ranks),
Memorial (scored expeditions), Help / Field Guide (this guide), Settings
and About (the version, credits and licences).
