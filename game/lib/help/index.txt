Ammerow Field Guide
===================

This is the in-game reference for Ammerow: Lands Beyond on a touch screen.
It covers the touch controls, the interface, surface travel, fishing, and
the turn-based side-view caves.  Every button presses one of the game's own
commands, so a hardware keyboard works too; section (g) lists its keys.

Tap a section to open it:

    (a) Touch controls
    (b) Map symbols
    (c) Interface, settings and saving
    (d) The known world and travel
    (e) Fishing
    (f) Side-view caves
    (g) Keyboard commands




Reading a page: PgDn and PgUp (or the d-pad) move through it, a tap on
the page advances it, Find searches it, and Back returns here or leaves
the guide.  The Help button closes the whole guide.
