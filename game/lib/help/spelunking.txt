Side-View Caves
===============

These caves are turn-based locations with their own movement rules.  A wrong
direction can step away from support and begin a real fall.  Read the stamina,
posture and Air display; animation never changes the resolved outcome.

The status strip along the bottom keeps HP, stamina, air and movement
separate from messages, with the cave section beneath them and, when
climbing, the stamina each direction costs.  It stays in view while
fishing.

Movement and safety
-------------------

D-pad               move, climb, descend, swim, or choose a jump direction
Centre of d-pad     wait one turn

The large button follows your situation:

  Jump    standing: begin the multi-stage jump (the d-pad's down after a
          jump abandons the reach and drops straight down)
  Grip    climbing, hanging, falling or at the top of a jump: cycle or set
          an adjacent grip target (free)
  Wait    swimming
  Get     pick up the objects here
  Up      take the passage up here; Down takes a passage down
  Leave   leave the cave by the way out under you

Around it are Grip (or Piton, when Grip is the large button), Rope and
Peek.  Piton hammers a piton at your position; Rope deploys rope downward
from the current piton.  Taps on the cave view do nothing: caves are moved
through with the d-pad.  More has every cave action under Caves.

Bare-rock gripping costs much more stamina than rope.  Stable ground allows
recovery by waiting; hanging from a piton does not.  A piton can support the
start of a jump, and a valid grip can arrest the rise or the held
ledge-reaching pause.  Diagonal grips and deliberate release are allowed.
Falls beyond a short safe distance deal sharply increasing damage and may
kill immediately.

From rope or a piton you can step diagonally up onto an adjoining
supported ledge without choosing a grip first.  A diagonal step down can
land on a piton or catch a rope; without support, you fall.  Hanging from
a piton still does not restore stamina.

Peek
----

Peek is not Look.  Keep one finger on Peek and press a direction on the
d-pad with another: you look one tile from a safe offset viewpoint without
moving.  Without Peek held, the same direction is a movement attempt.
Lifting your finger returns the viewpoint while keeping legitimately seen
cells.  Ordinary sight shows only the first part of a drop; a downward
diagonal Peek can reveal a long clear shaft and its enclosing rock faces.
Rock still blocks creatures, objects and open space around corners.
Menus and Back clear Peek.

Shared equipment and survival
-----------------------------

Inv, Char and the More drawer work here as they do above ground: wield,
take off, inscribe, refuel, drop, eat, quaff, read, use devices, activate,
browse, study, cast, fire and throw.  Look uses the cave-native Look
cursor.

Spellbooks must be carried.  Studying remains possible for every normal spell,
including one whose effect only works outside a side-view cave.  Casting keeps
the normal focus cost, failure chance and turn cost.  Aimed spells use the
eight directions of the d-pad; they collide with cave rock and creatures.
Effects without side-view meaning are refused before focus or a turn is
spent.

Scuba gear supplies a visible reserve underwater.  When it is exhausted,
ordinary Breath drains until you surface.  Surface recovery does not recharge
the gear.

Commands which still need side-view spatial rules are refused rather than
silently using a top-down target.  These include conventional targeting,
opening, closing, tunnelling, disarming, the ordinary dungeon-map view, nearby
monster/item lists, and multi-turn Rest.  Cave Survey and cave-native Look are
the deliberate side-view alternatives to the two map screens.
